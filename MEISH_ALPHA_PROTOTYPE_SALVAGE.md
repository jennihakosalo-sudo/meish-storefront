## PAGE-STRUCTURE LESSONS

Reusable **Meish page pattern** (intelligence, not HTML):

1. One page-specific hero (one sentence job).  
2. One strong proposition.  
3. Plain explanation (what we do **not** do).  
4. Process in few verbs.  
5. What Meish notices (concrete units).  
6. Small tool/example (taste, not the whole library).  
7. Operating principle / honesty limit.  
8. One next step (mail or one real offer).

Why it works: the visitor never has to learn the Universe to complete the page.
