Clarity Starter

A one-page worksheet to see what should become better — before you add a tool, a process, or AI. Keep it. Use it alone. No signup required.

How to use this gift

  1. Take about 10 quiet minutes. Phone away if you can.
  2. Write one short answer on the line under each question.
  3. Circle one next move below. That is your first step for this week.
1. What should become better here?
One sentence about your work, business, or everyday life.
 
2. For whom does it need to become better?
Name the people — yourself, customers, team, family…
 
3. What are you currently doing instead?
Or avoiding.
 
4. What creates the most unnecessary effort, waiting, or uncertainty?
 
5. If one thing changed this week, which change would free the most time or calm?
